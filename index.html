01 / WHO WE ARE

Curiosity is
our starting point.

BOVA is an independent research and development initiative focused on understanding the technology behind games.

We investigate undocumented formats, explore client and asset structures, and turn complex findings into practical knowledge. No unnecessary hype. Just research, experiments, and the drive to understand how things work.
